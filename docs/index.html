What does the score mean?

The number ranks which fictional methods may be worth researching. It is not a probability of success or evidence that a tactic generated revenue. A real decision still requires credible source material, a defined test, permissions and later measurement. In this version, there is no live monitoring or autonomous campaign execution.

The commercial question: If this test works, how will we know it improved relevant visibility, qualified inquiries, sales opportunities or pipeline rather than merely producing activity?
